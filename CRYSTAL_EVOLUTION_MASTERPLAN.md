## 8. Human + AI Collaboration

The collaboration model is:
- humans define intent, values, and accountability,
- AI accelerates understanding and option quality.

Principles:
1. AI supports judgment, never replaces ownership.
2. Human teams challenge AI when context disagrees.
3. Accepted recommendations become institutional learning.
4. Rejected recommendations improve governance quality.
5. Transparency and explanation are mandatory.

## 9. What Humans Should Always Decide

1. Safety-critical interventions.
2. Customer trust recovery and sensitive communications.
3. Final trade-offs between margin and relationship value.
4. Exceptions to standard policies.
5. Role progression and responsibility expansion.
6. Acceptance or rejection of new company practices.
7. Ethical boundaries for automation use.
8. Strategic direction and expansion priorities.
9. Disciplinary decisions and performance consequences.
10. Crisis decisions with legal or reputational impact.

## 10. What AI Should Always Recommend

1. Relevant historical cases for current incidents.
2. Risk flags for recurring patterns.
3. Prioritized next-best actions by role.
4. Operational bottleneck diagnostics.
5. Knowledge gaps where no validated practice exists.
6. Customer communication clarity improvements.
7. Probability-based workload and capacity imbalances.
8. Preventive actions before known failure windows.
9. Candidate practices ready for validation review.
10. Signals that require human escalation.

## 11. What AI Should Never Decide

1. Final decisions in safety-critical operations.
2. Disciplinary or career-impact judgments.
3. Commercial concessions without human approval.
4. Customer conflict resolution outcomes.
5. Ethical exceptions to policy.
6. Acceptance of unvalidated practices as official.
7. Strategic business direction changes.
8. Trust-sensitive communications without human review.
9. Any decision with unclear evidence basis.
10. Any decision where accountability cannot be assigned to a human owner.
