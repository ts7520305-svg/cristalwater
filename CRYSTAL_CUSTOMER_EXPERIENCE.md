# CRYSTAL CUSTOMER EXPERIENCE

Mission ID: UX-REBUILD-MASTER
Status: Strategy only. No implementation.

## Experience Goal
Deliver a premium, simple, trust-first customer portal.

## Core Promise
The customer immediately sees:
- Pool status
- Upcoming visit
- Invoices
- Payments
- Photos
- Reports
- Messages
Everything else stays hidden unless requested.

## Design Tone
- Elegant, calm, premium.
- Minimal jargon.
- Visual trust through spacing, clarity, and proof.

## Customer Home
- Health/Status summary
- Next visit card
- Payment status card
- Latest service photos
- Message CTA

## Screen Contract
- Where am I?
  - Home > Customer Summary
- What requires my attention?
  - due payments, upcoming visit confirmations, critical notices
- What is my next action?
  - confirm visit / pay invoice / contact team

## Daily Workflow
1. Check pool status
2. Confirm next visit
3. Review latest service proof
4. Resolve invoice/payment actions
5. Send message if needed

## Primary Actions
- Confirm upcoming visit
- Pay invoice or register payment
- Open latest report
- Send message

## Secondary Actions
- Download historical documents
- Update contact preferences
- Review full service timeline

## Information Hierarchy
1. Trust and status
2. Immediate action needed
3. Evidence (photos/reports)
4. History

## Notifications
- Upcoming visit reminder
- Service completed with proof
- Payment due/overdue
- Critical pool notice

## Reports and History
- Service timeline with media
- Invoice/payment timeline
- Communication timeline
- Download center

## End-of-Day Customer Flow
- Clear all pending confirmations/payments/messages
- Return to "all clear" state

## Device Behavior
- Mobile-first customer journey
- Tablet and desktop keep same hierarchy with more breathing room

## Premium UX Rules
- No operational clutter.
- No dense tables as default.
- One dominant action per section.
- High-quality visual rhythm and microcopy precision.
