# CRYSTAL SECRETARY EXPERIENCE

Mission ID: UX-REBUILD-MASTER
Status: Strategy only. No implementation.

## Experience Goal
Build the fastest coordination interface in Crystal OS.

## Core Philosophy
- Speed over exploration.
- Keyboard-first by default.
- Every frequent action reachable with shortcuts and command palette.

## Secretary Home
- Today appointments and confirmations
- Inbox requiring response
- Payments and reminders queue
- Unresolved service requests

## Screen Contract
- Where am I?
  - Today > Coordination Brief
- What requires my attention?
  - Missed confirmations, overdue callbacks, unpaid urgent invoices
- What is my next action?
  - Suggested action by priority queue

## Daily Workflow
1. Confirm agenda
2. Process inbound requests
3. Create/adjust visits
4. Trigger invoice/payment reminders
5. Escalate blockers to supervisor/admin
6. Prepare tomorrow schedule

## Primary Actions
- Quick Search (global)
- Quick Create Customer
- Quick Schedule Visit
- Quick Create Invoice
- Quick Send Reminder

## Secondary Actions
- Edit client details
- Attach documents
- Tag special handling notes

## Keyboard and Speed Layer
- Cmd/Ctrl+K: command palette
- Cmd/Ctrl+F: global search
- N then C: new customer
- N then V: new visit
- N then I: new invoice
- R then P: send payment reminder

## Interaction Design Rules
- Inline forms for frequent actions.
- Avoid full-screen forms for routine tasks.
- Smart defaults and templates in every quick flow.
- Keep typing minimal with suggestions/autofill.

## Notifications
- Queue grouped by urgency:
  - Needs response now
  - Due today
  - Upcoming

## History and Reports
- Communication timeline by customer
- Scheduling efficiency
- Reminder conversion
- No-show analysis

## End-of-Day Workflow
1. Confirm tomorrow schedule completeness
2. Empty urgent inbox
3. Handoff unresolved issues
4. Publish coordination summary

## Device Behavior
- Desktop: keyboard-accelerated dense mode.
- Tablet: split list/detail for calls and scheduling.
- Mobile: triage + quick update mode.
